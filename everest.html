Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a purchase?

Select any product, view details, click "Buy Now" or add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, fill out your delivery info, select payment method, and place your order.

How do I add items to the cart?

Click the "Add to Cart" button on any product card or in the product detail view. Your cart icon will update with the item count.

Where can I view my cart?

Click the cart icon in the top-right header to open the sliding cart drawer where you can view, update quantities, or remove items.

How do I search for products?

Use the search bar in the header to search by product name, category, or brand. You can also browse by clicking category tabs below the header.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), eSewa/Khalti digital wallets, and Credit/Debit Card payments.

How do I return a product?

You can return products within 7 days of delivery. Contact our support team at support@everestmart.com to initiate a return.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, all payment transactions are encrypted and securely processed. We never store your full card details on our servers.
